The insight iceberg, in full.

The deeper you go, the stronger the insight.

Insights are like ice;

Once exposed, they start melting.

A true insight has to be a revelation,

Something that people have not heard before.

To find them, you have to dive deep into the iceberg, where the thoughts are raw and rare.

However, the further down you go, the murkier it gets;

And knowing if you have an insight is harder to tell.

I made this visual to help you think of some new sources for where to find insights, check it out!
