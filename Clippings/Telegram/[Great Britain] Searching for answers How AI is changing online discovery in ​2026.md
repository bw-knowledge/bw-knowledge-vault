AI assistants are becoming a bigger part of how people find information online, but have they supplanted search engines as the go-to option for online queries?

In YouGov’s _Great Britain web search and AI report 2026_, we explore how consumers are using AI assistants alongside traditional search, where AI fits into the search journey, and what still holds people back from relying on it.

Drawing on a YouGov survey of 2,000 British adults, the report reveals how search habits are evolving, where AI delivers the most value, and why trust remains the defining challenge for AI-powered search.
