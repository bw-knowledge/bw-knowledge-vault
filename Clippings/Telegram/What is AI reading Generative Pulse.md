## Headlines

- **AI systems rely heavily on earned media for citations in consumer queries.** Analysis of millions of prompts shows earned media drives the majority of sources cited by AI when answering consumer questions about meal kits, travel rewards, and similar topics.
- **Citation behavior varies significantly across different AI providers.** Some AI providers link to unique domains more frequently than others, suggesting different sourcing strategies or training data compositions.
- **AI responses lack consistency in source selection for identical queries.** The same prompt can produce different cited sources across multiple runs, indicating variability in how AI systems retrieve and prioritize information.
