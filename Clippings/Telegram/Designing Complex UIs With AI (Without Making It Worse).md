In summary, designing complex UIs with AI requires context engineering and guided AI systems that respect boundaries and user workflows, rather than deploying unguided AI that produces generic outcomes.

## Headlines

- **Context engineering makes complex systems manageable without oversimplifying them.** AI-aided design requires structured information architecture that guides AI outputs within appropriate boundaries, particularly for enterprise software and legacy environments.

- **Guided AI produces contextually appropriate results while unguided AI delivers generic outcomes.** The distinction lies in building decision infrastructure that constrains AI behavior to respect domain-specific constraints and user needs.

- **Prioritize AI features by severity, frequency, frustration, and risk rather than rushing implementations.** High-usage, high-repetition, low-risk tasks represent the best candidates for AI integration in complex product environments.

- **Task analysis and user workflow understanding form the foundation for effective AI-assisted design.** Understanding how users navigate complex, non-linear systems prevents AI from introducing accidental complexity alongside essential complexity.
