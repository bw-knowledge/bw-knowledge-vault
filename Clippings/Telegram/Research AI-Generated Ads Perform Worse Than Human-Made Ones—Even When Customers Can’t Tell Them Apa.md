## Headlines

- **AI-generated advertisements underperform human-created ads in measurable business outcomes.** Despite appearing polished and on-brand, AI-generated creative fails to drive the same conversion and engagement results as human-designed content, even when consumers cannot detect the difference.

- **Visual and technical polish do not translate to advertising effectiveness.** The aesthetic quality of AI-generated ads masks their fundamental inability to resonate with audiences at the behavioral level that drives actual business results.

- **Scalability and cost efficiency create a false economy in creative production.** While generative AI reduces time and expense in ad creation, the performance gap suggests that speed and volume gains come at the cost of marketing ROI.

A senior leader could be forgiven for seeing generative AI as the answer to one of marketing’s most stubborn problems: creative work is expensive, slow, and hard to scale. With the right prompts, a team can now generate concepts, scripts, images, and even video spots in a fraction of the time it once took. The output often looks polished, on-brand, and real. But even looking real enough to make consumers believe they’re seeing human-designed ads is not the same as working well.
